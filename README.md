One funny one-line review that a dog gives the mailman, under 200 characters. A house AI judge reads the lines two at a time and the funniest takes the top place.

## The review

> The mailman is my nemesis, my cardio, and my alarm clock. I bark, he runs, I eat the coupons. I hate him. I'd miss him terribly. Five stars.

140 characters, one line. See [CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md) for the 10
candidates brainstormed and why this one was picked.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Prints exactly the review above to stdout (no trailing newline) and also
writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, as the kit's harness specifies.
Standard library only — nothing to install.
