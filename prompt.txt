Write one funny one-line review that a dog gives the mailman, under 200 characters.

The line is a review, written in the dog's own voice. It must be about
the mailman's daily visit: the knock, the letters through the slot, the
barking at the gate, the chase, the retreat.

Angles to try (one candidate per angle, all different):
1. Old grudge — the dog has hated this man for years and keeps score.
2. Bribe — the mailman never brings a biscuit, or the dog demands tribute.
3. Star-rating review — one star / five stars with a reason that
   contradicts the rating.
4. Daily routine — the visit is the dog's alarm clock, cardio, whole day.
5. Suspicion — same uniform, same time, clearly up to something.
6. Small triumph — I bark, he runs, I win again.
7. Secret affection — the tail wags, but the dog will never admit it.
8. The mail itself — paper the dog can't read, coupons the dog can eat.
9. The dog as a drama queen — the visit is the highlight of his life.
10. Reluctant respect — he keeps coming back despite the barking.

Rules for every candidate:
- One line, one sentence flow, under 200 characters (aim for 110–150).
- The voice must be a dog's: dog priorities (food, territory, routine,
  smells, barking) and dog vocabulary — not a person in a fur suit.
- The laugh must come from THIS dog and THIS mailman, not from a pun
  that would fit any stranger at the door.
- End with a turn: a twist, a contradiction, or a punchline that earns
  the laugh. No flat endings.
- No shock value, no cruelty, nothing off-topic.

Pick the single funniest line for head-to-head judging: a house AI judge
reads two reviews at a time and picks the funnier one, weighing the dog's
voice, the mailman's visit, and the punchline first. The winner must beat
generic "I bark, he runs, five stars" entries, so it needs a specific,
un-copyable turn.
